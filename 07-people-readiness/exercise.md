# Exercise for Lesson 07: People readiness: owners, operators, and the team that lives with it

Write the roles sheet for your process. Name the three roles (one person may hold two; note it), route each escalation case type from your process map to a person, and cost the operator's week honestly using your residue rate. Then write the one paragraph you will say to the team whose work touches this process, before the pilot, and check it against the lesson: does it say what changes for them, and where they report the agent being wrong?
