# Solution for Lesson 07: People readiness: owners, operators, and the team that lives with it

BrightSmile's sheet in the artifact rewards a second look for its least technical line: the Saturday phone hour trade. Readiness documents are full of names; the trade is what makes this one true, because the operator's thirty minutes had to come from somewhere and the sheet says where. The backup operator line teaches the companion habit: readiness includes the calendar. Holidays, sick days, and the busy season are known in advance, and an agent supervised by exactly one person is a single point of failure wearing a lanyard. Finally, note that the vendor appears exactly once, as a technical escalation route with the owner copied: vendors are part of the system, never the supervision of it.
