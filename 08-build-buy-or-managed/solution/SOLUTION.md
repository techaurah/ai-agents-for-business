# Solution for Lesson 08: Build versus buy versus managed service: the fair comparison

The worked totals (managed 40, buy 33, build 27) matter less than two structural features of the table. First, the heaviest weight sits on maintenance capability, because that is where small business agent projects actually die, in month four, when the prompts need changing and nobody can. Second, managed loses points exactly where its critics would predict, exit cost and data location, and wins anyway on this fact pattern; a fair comparison that never lets your preferred route lose a criterion is a rationalisation, and the exercise of scoring against your favourite is the discipline this lesson exists to teach. If your scores come out tied, that is information too: choose by the sensitivity sentence, and record it.
