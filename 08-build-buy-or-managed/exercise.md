# Exercise for Lesson 08: Build versus buy versus managed service: the fair comparison

Score your own three routes. Write your fact pattern in five lines first (process standard or unusual, technical skills on staff, readiness grades, change frequency, data constraints), then adapt the criteria and weights, and score honestly. Finish with the sensitivity sentence: the answer flips to route X if fact Y changes. File both; the capstone requires the decision record, not just the decision.
