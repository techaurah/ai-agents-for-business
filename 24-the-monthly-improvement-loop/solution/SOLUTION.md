# Solution for Lesson 24: The monthly improvement loop

The worked review models the loop's temperament: mostly confirmations, one small rung change, and a scope request handled by routing rather than by enthusiasm or refusal. Notice the rung change's size, twenty operator minutes a week. That is what real agent improvement looks like after the launch headlines fade: not transformations, but a supervised system getting measurably less burdensome at a rate the scorecard can see. Notice also what did not happen: nobody edited the agent's prompts in a chat thread at 10pm, and the provider's export was requested on a calm Tuesday. The loop is boring in the way that drains are boring, right up until the month it is the only thing between the business and a very interesting incident.
