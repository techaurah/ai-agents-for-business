# Exercise for Lesson 03: Where agents fail: the honest no list

Apply the filter to your Lesson 02 ranking and to at least three jobs nobody proposed but an enthusiastic vendor might add later (complaints, refunds, advice in your field's sensitive area). Produce your no list in the artifact's format, one line per job with its failed test, and mark at least one job assist only, with a sentence on what the human still decides there.
