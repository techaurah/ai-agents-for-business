# Solution for Lesson 13: Baselines and success metrics: measure before you automate

The worked sheet's instructive figure is not the median response time, which everyone expected to be bad, but the never answered rate, which nobody at BrightSmile believed until the count. That is the baseline's deeper function: it replaces the business's folk model of its own process with a measured one, and several later arguments (was the agent worth it, did quality drop, who is busier now) quietly resolve back to this sheet. Note the correctness target, set at the human sample's level rather than at perfection: the pilot's honest question is parity or better at speed, and a target of 100 percent would be a decision to stop disguised as a standard, as Lesson 22 will show when the results land near, not on, the line.
