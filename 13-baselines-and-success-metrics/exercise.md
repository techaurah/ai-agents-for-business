# Exercise for Lesson 13: Baselines and success metrics: measure before you automate

Take your baseline. Over the next two ordinary weeks (or retrospectively from timestamps and records, labelled as such), fill the sheet: volume, first response median and tail, never handled rate, standard path share, sampled correctness on twenty cases, and baseline cost per handled unit. Then set scale and stop thresholds for each pilot measure and have the owner sign the sheet, literally or in your decision file.
