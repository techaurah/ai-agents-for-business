# Exercise for Lesson 14: The agent job description: task, tools, limits, tone

Write the job description for your pilot agent, on one page, in the artifact's shape. Draft the nevers from your no list and access plan first, then scope from your map, then write the three tone examples yourself before letting anyone, human or model, draft alternatives. Finish by reading it as three people: a builder pricing it, a vendor being held to it, and an operator reviewing a bad case against it. Fix what each reading exposes.
