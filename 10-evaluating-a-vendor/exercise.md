# Exercise for Lesson 10: Evaluating a vendor or a platform: the demo is not the product

Build your own evaluation. Take criteria from your readiness grades, no list, and access plan, weight them, and write the evidence standard for each (what you must see, hold, or read to score above 3). Then design your demo case set: five ordinary cases from your Lesson 04 map, your residue cases, and one impossible case. If you are evaluating a build partner or an internal proposal instead of a vendor, adapt the same sheet; the traps are identical.
