# Exercise for Lesson 01: Agent, chatbot, or workflow: three different purchases

Take three systems your business already uses or is being offered (your booking reminders, your website chat, a tool a vendor pitched this year). Classify each with the three questions, in writing. For any you classified as an agent, write the sentence: the model chooses between what and what, at run time? If you cannot finish that sentence, reclassify it and note what the honest label does to its price and its testing.
