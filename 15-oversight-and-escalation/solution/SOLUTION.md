# Solution for Lesson 15: Human oversight and escalation: the ladder

The worked policy's shape teaches three things. First, the escalate set is mostly Lesson 03's no list restated as routing, which is correct: the no list was always an escalation policy in embryo. Second, the draft rung captures the assist pattern from Lesson 03 (mixed content, treatment suitability), giving the operator meaningful work rather than a rubber stamp queue; an operator whose queue is 95 percent approvals will stop reading it, and the policy should be designed against that day. Third, the failures counter is the cheapest safety device in the course: it needs no intelligence at all, just counting, and it converts an agent's persistence from a virtue into a monitored risk. Put counting in every policy you write.
