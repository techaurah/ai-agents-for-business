# Exercise for Lesson 15: Human oversight and escalation: the ladder

Write your escalation policy and encode it in the artifact's shape. List every action from your job description, place each on a rung with its reason, define the escalation context pack, and set response time standards per rung that your rota can actually meet. Then run your Lesson 02 residue cases and three invented hard cases through it, and fix every gap or double claim the run exposes.
