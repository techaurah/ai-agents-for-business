# Solution for Lesson 18: The failure catalogue: how agents go wrong in businesses

The worked catalogue's balance is worth noticing: four entries owned by the operator, four by the owner, and the split is by kind, containment versus consequence. The operator owns what must move in the first hour; the owner owns what involves customers being told, scope being judged, or money being committed. The silent stall entry carries the subtlest detection in the set, the heartbeat check, because its symptom is an absence: no queue, no escalations, no errors, on a day when enquiries certainly arrived. Teach your operator the sentence: an empty queue is a finding, not a relief, until the arrivals count reconciles. More pilots are saved by that sentence than by any model improvement in this course.
