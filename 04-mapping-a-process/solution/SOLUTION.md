# Solution for Lesson 04: Mapping a process before you automate it

BrightSmile's map in the artifact is the worked example, and its three decisions preview the whole design. Decision one (standard path or not) becomes the agent's triage rule and the core of its job description. Decision two (clinical or emotional goes to the manager) becomes the first line of the escalation policy, note that it is a handover with a named human, not a dead end. Decision three (chase once, then stop) becomes policy too, and a taste rule as much as a logic rule: a business that chases three times has made a values choice the map should surface. The map's headline finding, eight hours of waiting in a nine minute process, is the honest business case: nobody's job is threatened by this agent, the queue is.
