# Exercise for Lesson 04: Mapping a process before you automate it

Map your own winning process from Lesson 02, from five real cases, with the person who does the job. Produce the map in the artifact's format (or on paper in the same shape): every stage in order, decisions with their rule of thumb, waits with durations, handovers named. Finish with two sentences: where the elapsed time actually goes, and which decision point has no writable rule. Keep the map; Lessons 12 to 15 each consume one part of it.
