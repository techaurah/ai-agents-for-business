# Exercise for Lesson 02: The opportunity audit: finding the work an agent can carry

Run the audit on your own business. List at least five processes, tally or honestly estimate weekly volume, sample twenty cases of your leading candidate and sort them into the paths staff actually took, then score all five with the artifact's method (edit the data or copy the method to paper). Bring two outputs to the next lesson: your ranking, and the list of cases from your sample that fit no path. The residue list is the most valuable page you will produce this module.
