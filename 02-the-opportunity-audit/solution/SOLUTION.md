# Solution for Lesson 02: The opportunity audit: finding the work an agent can carry

Worked example continued in the artifact: BrightSmile's ranking is enquiry response (score 12 on the stated inputs), then recall reminders on volume alone, review handling, invoice chasing, and treatment explanations last by a wide margin. The sample of twenty enquiries sorted into: book a checkup (9), price or treatment information (6), insurance and payment questions (3), and two that fit no path, a complaint about parking with a booking buried in it, and a parent asking about a child's tooth pain. Those two residue cases are not a problem for the audit; they are the design brief for the escalation policy in Lesson 15. An agent that handles the eighteen and hands over the two, gracefully and fast, is the whole course in miniature.
