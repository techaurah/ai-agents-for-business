# Exercise for Lesson 29: Ship it: present, publish, and showcase your plan

Run the full ship routine on your plan: clean copy validation, the ninety second test on a real stranger if you can recruit one, the numbers trace, the data sweep, the meeting, the recorded decision. Then publish the plan in your fork and, if you wish, submit it to the showcase by the instructions in showcase/README.md, one row, honestly described. This is the final exercise of the course; there is no solution to compare against except the gates themselves, which is rather the point.
