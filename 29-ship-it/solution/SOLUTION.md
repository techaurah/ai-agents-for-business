# Solution for Lesson 29: Ship it: present, publish, and showcase your plan

The worked example's instructive detail is the first page rewrite. BrightSmile's draft led with the chosen provider, because the provider had dominated the project's recent weeks, and the ninety second test exposed what that ordering confessed: the plan had drifted from a decision document into a purchase justification. The rewrite, process, question, route, gate date, took twenty minutes and changed how the partners read everything after it, since a plan that starts with the business's own question invites scrutiny of evidence, while one that starts with a product invites scrutiny of the salesperson. If your plan survives its checks but fails the stranger test, believe the stranger. They are reading the plan the way month nine will.
