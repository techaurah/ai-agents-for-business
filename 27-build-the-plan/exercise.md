# Exercise for Lesson 27: Build the plan: readiness, pilot, oversight

Build your plan now, in the lesson's order, from your skeleton. Run the repository validator on your plan file, then perform the four cross checks (by hand or by adapting the artifact) and record every finding and its fix in a build log appended to the plan. Do not proceed to Lesson 28 with a known contradiction unfixed; a labelled gap with a date is acceptable, a contradiction is not.
