# Exercise for Lesson 20: Testing before trust: the acceptance set

Build your acceptance set. Start from the artifact's categories and write at least four cases per category from your own materials: your map's ordinary paths, your residue list, hostile versions of your real enquiries, your tone examples, your data boundaries, and two drills from your failure catalogue. State each case's expected handling in ladder language, then run the set, or have your provider run it, and file the scored result.
