# Solution for Lesson 28: Stress test the plan: red team your own deployment

The worked sheet's two gaps share a moral worth extracting. Both were quiet, internal, and well intentioned: a helpful staff member refreshing data, a feed failing silently. Neither involved an attacker, a model error, or a vendor failure, the three things plans instinctively defend. That is the stress test's deepest lesson for owners: the plan's enemies are mostly entropy and kindness, and the defences are mostly edit rights, reconciliations, and written only rules, the unglamorous furniture of operations. If your stress test finds its gaps in the same furniture, it has worked. File the sheet, fixes and all, because Lesson 29's reviewers will trust a plan whose stress record shows scars far more than one claiming a clean pass.
