# Exercise for Lesson 19: Pilot simulation: the lead response twin

Adapt the twin to your process: your audited volume and standard path share, your policy's escalation and draft rates, your rota's real minutes and per case handling time. Run the three settings and record, in the pilot file, where the rota breaks and the surge rule you adopt in response. If your numbers clear every stress, distrust the inputs before you celebrate the design; recheck the residue rate against a fresh sample.
